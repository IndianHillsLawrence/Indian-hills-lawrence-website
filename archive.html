President 1 Leads the board and runs board and general meetings. Serves as the main spokesperson for the neighborhood with the City, partner organizations and the media.
President-Elect 0 Supports the President and fills in when the President is unavailable. Becomes President the following year.
Secretary 1 Records and distributes meeting minutes, keeps official records and bylaws, and sends meeting notices.
Treasurer 1 Manages the association's funds, including dues, donations, budget and bank accounts. Gives financial reports at meetings.
Communications Director 0 Manages the newsletter, website, social media and email updates.
Events & Social Chair 0 Plans neighborhood gatherings, welcomes new residents and recruits volunteers.
Safety & City Liaison 0 Connects the neighborhood with the city, LAN and local agencies on traffic, zoning and public safety.
Beautification & Green Space Chair 1 Leads projects that improve shared spaces, such as entrance signs, plantings and trees.

Neighborhood Fall Festival — October 17

Archived October 20, 2026

The Indian Hills Neighborhood was invited to a Neighborhood Fall Festival on Saturday, October 17, 2026, starting at 4 p.m.

This outdoor event included food (hot dogs and a variety of side dishes), family-oriented games (relay races, cornhole, a basketball shooting contest, and more), and spaces for sitting and visiting.

The event was hosted by Southside Church of Christ at 1105 W. 25th Street (25th and Missouri), where neighbors come to vote.
