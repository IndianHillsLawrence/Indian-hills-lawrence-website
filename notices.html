October 22, 2026 - Indian Hills Neighbors: We Want to Hear From You!

Sent October 22, 2026

The Indian Hills Neighborhood Association (IHNA) is working to revitalize neighborhood participation and build a stronger voice for our community. Whether you rent or own, have lived here for decades, or just moved in recently, your perspective matters.

IHNA is not an HOA. Membership is completely voluntary, there are no mandatory dues, and we have no authority over your property. Our goal is simple: bring neighbors together, organize community events, share information, and help give Indian Hills a unified voice on issues like parks, traffic, safety, and neighborhood improvements.

You do not need to volunteer or attend meetings to participate. If all you can do right now is complete the survey and occasionally stay informed, we still want to hear from you. The association can only represent the neighborhood if the neighborhood is heard.

Neighborhood Survey

Please take a few minutes to complete our neighborhood survey.

October 10, 2026 - Neighborhood Fall Festival

Sent October 10, 2026

The Indian Hills Neighborhood is invited to a Neighborhood Fall Festival on Saturday, October 17, starting at 4:00 p.m.

This outdoor event will include food (hot dogs and a variety of side dishes), family-oriented games (relay races, cornhole, basketball shooting contests, and more), and plenty of space for sitting and visiting with neighbors. Please dress for the weather.

The event is hosted by the Southside Church of Christ at 1105 W. 25th Street (25th and Missouri), the same location where many neighborhood residents vote.

You can view the full announcement on our neighborhood website at Indian Hills Neighborhood Association.

Residents are welcome to submit neighborhood events and announcements for consideration. To request review for distribution through the ListServ and/or posting on the website, please email indianhillslawrence@gmail.com.
