Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 18, 2026. Last updated: November 19, 2026.

The Indian Hills Neighborhood Association (IHNA) respects your privacy. This policy explains how IHNA handles information when you visit indianhillslawrence.us or use the communication services linked from the website.

Information you provide

If you email IHNA, request to join the mailing list, or complete a linked Google Form, the information you provide may include your name, email address, street address, message, survey answers, or other information requested by that form. Form fields vary; please review each form before submitting.

Mailing-list requests are submitted through a Google Form. A list manager reviews requests and manually adds approved subscribers to the Google Groups list. You do not need to sign in to Google to request to join. You may unsubscribe using the instructions in a list email. Messages sent to the list may be distributed to its subscribers; do not send private information to the group.

Information processed when you visit the site

The website is hosted with GitHub Pages. GitHub says it logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes. See GitHub Pages information and GitHub's privacy statement.

The website uses GoatCounter to count page views. Depending on enabled metrics, GoatCounter may store aggregate page-view and referrer counts, as well as broad browser, operating system, approximate location, language, and screen-width statistics. Its individual-pageview feature is disabled by default but can be enabled in site settings. GoatCounter says it processes the site name, IP address, and User-Agent in memory for up to eight hours to identify repeat visits, but does not store raw IP addresses or full User-Agent strings in its database and does not use browser cookies or local storage. The website code does not send Google Forms responses to GoatCounter. See GoatCounter's privacy documentation.

The homepage embeds a public Google Calendar. When the calendar is loaded, your browser connects to Google to retrieve calendar content and may share technical request information such as your IP address and browser details. Google handles that information under its own policies. See Google's privacy policy.

How IHNA uses and shares information

IHNA may use information you provide to:

  • Review mailing-list requests and manage neighborhood communications.
  • Respond to questions, event submissions, and other messages.
  • Summarize survey results and understand neighborhood concerns.

IHNA does not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Google, GitHub, and GoatCounter process information as needed to provide their services, subject to their own policies and settings. IHNA may disclose information when required by law.

Survey results may be summarized without identifying individuals. IHNA will not publish identifying details from a personal submission without permission. IHNA does not promise that form responses are anonymous; review the form settings and questions before submitting.

Cookies and external services

IHNA does not set advertising or cross-site tracking cookies through its website code. GoatCounter's documented setup does not use browser cookies or local storage. The embedded Google Calendar and external Google Forms or other websites may use cookies or similar technologies under their own policies and settings.

Your choices and contact information

You may unsubscribe from the mailing list using the instructions in a list email. To ask IHNA to update or delete information it controls, email indianhillslawrence@gmail.com. Information held by Google, GitHub, or another provider is also subject to that provider's systems and policies and may not be immediately removable by IHNA.
