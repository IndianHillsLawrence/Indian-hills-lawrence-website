Join the Indian Hills mailing list

Google Groups manages membership for this private mailing list. You must sign in to a Google Account and complete the Join group step before you are subscribed.

  1. Select Continue joining the mailing list.
  2. If you see “Content unavailable,” select Sign in and sign in to a Google Account. Then return here and select the button again.
  3. On the group page, select Join group and follow Google's prompts to finish joining.

You do not need a Gmail address. If you do not have a Google Account, create one with Google. You can use an existing non-Gmail email address when creating the account. Then return here and select the button below to continue joining the mailing list.
